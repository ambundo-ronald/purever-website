Solution-diffusion model

Water flux J = A·TCF·(ΔP − Δπ). Salt flux Js = B·TCF·ΔC. Concentration polarisation and the log-mean concentrate salinity are included.

Temperature correction

Membrane permeability rises about 3% per °C: TCF = exp[2640·(1/298 − 1/T)]. Warm water produces more permeate at slightly higher salt passage.

Energy & recovery

Specific energy is calculated from pump hydraulics at 80% efficiency. The isobaric ERD returns about 95% of the concentrate’s pressure energy to the feed.
